The transparent WebP sheets use 384 px cells. The seated sheets have front and rear rows. The in-cave renderer mirrors the three-quarter poses to match the view. The carrying sequence plays during Bera’s actual transfers.

## Generation prompts

Each seated sheet used the same prompt with the character description appended. The corresponding Family Tree portrait was the image reference.

> Create a transparent game sprite atlas as an exact 3-column, 2-row grid, six completely separate full-body illustrations of THE SAME person from reference image. Identity, clothing colors, hairstyle, ochre dry-gouache faceted style and broad umber outlines are essential. Keep all six figures full body, sitting low cross-legged at the SAME spot with hands visible and same head size and consistent proportions, with clear transparent gutters, each figure centered within one equal rectangular cell and never touching its bounds. No backgrounds, props, ground planes, words, frames, or dividers. Every pose must fit in its own cell. Top row, left to right: (1) faces viewer front three-quarter, looks up, hands resting in lap; (2) front three-quarter, eyes down, raises right hand a little to work; (3) front three-quarter eyes down, right arm reaches DOWN and OUT from body at a low imaginary working surface, left hand stabilizes at the knee. Bottom row, left to right: (4) matching rear three-quarter of pose 1, seen clearly from behind, same silhouette; (5) matching rear three-quarter of pose 2 with raised right arm, clearly seen from behind; (6) matching rear three-quarter of pose 3 with working arm reaching outward and down, clearly seen from behind. Prehistoric hide clothing down to knees, bare arms, rough simple foot wraps. Broad faceted painted shapes and a few pale scratches exactly like the portrait. Opaque, clean silhouettes over genuine transparency. These are animation poses, not different people. Character:

- **bera** — Bera, strong adult man with tawny laced hide, rough dark beard and grey at the temples
- **nara** — Nara, adult woman, wavy dark hair knotted behind, ochre wrap and reddish brown hide apron
- **ina** — Ina, mature woman, square brows, short dark hair with small bone pin, charcoal brown working apron
- **mira** — Mira, younger woman with two tied dark side strands, fawn hide shoulder wrap

Bera’s carrying sequence used his portrait and this prompt.

> Create one transparent sprite atlas with three equal columns, one row. The adult prehistoric man is Bera, exactly matching the reference portrait's dark beard, greying temples and torn tobacco-colored hide. Three full-body rear three-quarter profile walking poses, moving toward the right with a cloth-wrapped bundle held in front (leave hands empty, shaped to support a load, the game will attach its actual prop). Feet planted on a common baseline, full body entirely inside each cell. Pose 1 right foot forward, pose 2 feet passing, pose 3 left foot forward. Same shoulders and head height. Ochre dry gouache, faceted planes and umber outlines as the portrait. Subtle asymmetry. No background, ground, outline glow, text, vignette, props, frames or labels. Genuine transparent gutters.
