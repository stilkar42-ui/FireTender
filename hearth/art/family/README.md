# Stone Mountain’s kin

The twelve WebP portraits in this directory were painted with the built-in ImageGen tool and resized for the game. The Fire Speaker and Rusk use the existing Firetender artwork in the hearth directory. The player character has no portrait.

## Generation prompts

Each request used the shared brief below, followed by its subject description.

> Use case illustration-story. Square 1:1 character portrait for the fictional Paleolithic cave game Firetender. One centered waist-up figure framed with 8% margins, roughly three-quarter view; head occupies upper half of image, readable face at small UI size. Style of angular dry-gouache and heavy deep umber ink silhouettes from a prehistoric illustrated game: broad sharply faceted matte geometric planes, bold dark outlines, a few broad pale ochre strokes, rich but restrained dark umber, tobacco, sandstone, terracotta, and warm firelit skin. Flat painted illustration with simple strong readable shapes, around two to four tonal facets per face, realistic human proportions and natural demeanor, coarse hide clothing with simple seams and sinew. Smooth muted very dark brown backdrop with a diffuse subtle umber glow, no other scene elements. No lettering, modern features, fantasy accessories, jewelry, digitally glossy shading, frames or props behind the figure.

- **Oren** — Older grandfather, lean long face, narrow eyes, irregular silver beard and thinning wavy gray hair, dark ochre wrap, holding a short notched piece of antler.
- **Dena** — Older grandmother, broad angular jaw, silver hair cropped roughly at the jaw, a deep russet fur-lined wrap, focused eyes and a strip of soft hide folded in one hand.
- **Nara** — Adult mother about forty, broad cheekbones and dark wavy shoulder-length hair tied back in a low knot, steady attentive expression, brown hide apron and short roasting fork.
- **Bera** — Adult father about forty, broad nose and dark shaggy hair with gray at temples, short dark beard, wide shoulders, plain ochre-tan hide shirt and wooden hunting spear.
- **Tavi** — Adult aunt about thirty-seven, oval broad-cheeked face, a sweep of dark hair tied behind one ear, lively watchful expression, umber hide shoulder wrap and pale roots gathered in her hands.
- **Ivo** — Adult uncle about forty, long nose, short curl of beard, curly dark brown hair, narrow smile, russet hide cape and loop of carrying thong.
- **Kett** — Adult uncle in his late thirties, broad jaw, broken-looking nose, rough short hair and dark stubble, slate brown hide shirt, stone-tipped wooden spear.
- **Ina** — Adult aunt in her late thirties, square-set brows, short coarse dark hair pinned with a sliver of bone, intent gaze, charcoal brown hide working apron, holding a small pale knapped point.
- **Mira** — Young adult cousin about twenty, dark wavy hair with two short tied strands, warm broad cheeks, deft serious expression, rough fawn hide shoulder wrap, a simple hide scraper.
- **Lio** — Boy about nine, round face, dark uneven fringe, eager expression, small tawny hide wrap and a bundle of thin dry kindling.
- **Suri** — Girl about seven, round cheeks and a chin-length dark crop with a small tuft tied with sinew, curious sideways glance, plain sienna hide wrap, two small pale stones.
