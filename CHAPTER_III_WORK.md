# Chapter III · The Work of the Family

Open **PLAY.cmd**, enter the hearth through the launch menu, and press **F8** for the Chapter III practice start. In the story, **Continue to Chapter III** carries your fire, fuel, and known territory forward from Chapter II. Press **Space** to resume or use **+2 seconds** for close work.

## The fallen bison

Once Oren returns from the ledge, listen for Rusk’s report. **Shelter news** and the grazing ground in the **F10** Mind Cave show what the family has heard. The elders weigh the path, the people available, and the backlog before sending a pair to carry back what they can. The damaged side stays behind. Loads arrive in stages and yield separate cuts, marrow bones, hide, fat and trimmings. Scavengers take some of what stays exposed.

One story day is six minutes of hearth time. Warmth and exposure shorten the time a cut keeps. The covered shelf and cooking give the family more time to use it. The steppe bison is a plausible choice for the story. Research reports bison in southwestern France during Marine Isotope Stage 4. [Southwestern France prey mobility study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0277379124001112)

## Your work at the hearth

Nara prepares the food, Bera processes the catch, and Ina prepares resin and repairs hunting tools. Watch for **Nara needs steady red** or **Ina needs indirect warmth**. Select the request to hear from the worker at their station. Tend the existing bed with fuel, air and placement. The specialist resumes when a usable spot is available. Bera can split bones and work cuts at a cold hearth.

## Learn and help

Choose a work area from the shelter menu or click its label. **Observe** leaves the specialist to work. **Learn** asks them to show the selected technique on a real piece. Afterward their portrait offers guidance while you try it. **Help** becomes available at Practiced. Choose a free piece and advance time while you work. You can also help when a bench is unattended. Press **Esc** with empty hands to leave the bench and **Home** to return the camera to the hearth.

Click or drag to lift a piece, then place it on a work surface. **I** inspects it, **V** applies the contextual action, and **Esc** returns a held item to its pickup spot. **Bench** opens the contextual action list. The **Techniques** tab in **F9** records the teacher, proficiency, materials, tools, conditions and signs for each learned process.

- **Extract marrow.** Set a long marrow bone on Bera’s working stone. Work it with the hammerstone and separate the marrow from the splinters.
- **Roast bison meat.** Size a fresh cut on Nara’s board. Place it beside steady coals, turn it to brown both faces, and lift it to the covered shelf when the middle is warm.
- **Prepare resin adhesive.** Scrape the knot on Ina’s stone and load the stone cup. Warm it gently and lift the cup when the resin runs evenly. Smoke signals that the resin is overheating.
- **Repair a hafted hunting tool.** Bring the spear to Ina. Prepare the seating faces, fit the point with adhesive and binding, and inspect the haft before returning it to the rack.

Use **G** to speak with Eren about what happened or what you did. **F5** opens the selected profile’s save slots and **F11** opens its load slots. A Chapter III save restores the living hearth, field recovery, workshop materials and apprenticeship.
